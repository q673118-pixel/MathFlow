# MathFlow 1.5.4

## Added
- Unit converter inside the existing Tools tab.
- Ten categories: length, mass, area, volume, temperature, speed, time, pressure, energy and data.
- Automatic conversion, unit swapping and offline operation.
- SI decimal and IEC binary data units are distinguished.

## Reliability
- BigDecimal-based conversion instead of binary floating-point arithmetic.
- Absolute-zero validation for temperature.
- Unit converter test coverage.
